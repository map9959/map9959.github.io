Hi! I'm Michael Paciullo, a software engineer at [WRITER](https://writer.com), building a generative AI platform for sales and marketing teams at Fortune 500 companies. I have past experience in numerical linear algebra, physical simulation, and computer graphics, and I am always looking to learn and build!

I started my career at [Standard Practice](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stevenaquino/2024/04/22/how-standard-practice-is-using-ai-to-make-administrative-tasks-more-accessible-to-healthcare-workers-everywhere/), building voice AI that calls insurance companies to get doctors paid quicker! Before that, I was an undergraduate researcher at NYU's Geometric Computing Lab, contributing to [polysolve](https://github.com/polyfem/polysolve), a linear and non-linear solver API for finite element method simulations. I also worked at the [Flatiron Institute](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/flatiron/), the research division of the Simons Foundation, implementing fast matrix factorization algorithms on GPUs.
