Michael Paciullo

Software Engineer at Writer | BA in CS/Math from NYU | Former intern @ Flatiron

Hi! I’m Michael Paciullo, a software engineer at WRITER, building a generative AI platform for sales and marketing teams at Fortune 500 companies. I have past experience in numerical linear algebra, physical simulation, and computer graphics, and I am always looking to learn and build!

I started my career at Standard Practice, building voice AI that calls insurance companies to get doctors paid quicker! Before that, I was an undergraduate researcher at NYU’s Geometric Computing Lab, contributing to polysolve, a linear and non-linear solver API for finite element method simulations. I also worked at the Flatiron Institute, the research division of the Simons Foundation, implementing fast matrix factorization algorithms on GPUs.

Feel free to connect with me through any of the social media links below. For any industry-related inquiries, please contact me via LinkedIn. For academic inquiries, feel free to email me.
